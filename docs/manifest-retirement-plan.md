# Manifest Retirement Plan

Manifest is being retired as an operational source of truth, not deleted in one destructive release.

The AI-native operating system reads canonical evidence. AI does not make an old JSON document authoritative: lifecycle, owner, consent, suppression, communication state, tasks, campaign membership, and dispositions remain durable governed records. Manifest may temporarily hold derived enrichment, historical seller context, and briefing inputs that do not yet have a canonical home.

## Current boundary

- Canonical systems win every conflict.
- No new product feature may depend on Manifest for operational or compliance authority.
- Lifecycle changes write canonically first; the existing Manifest update is a non-blocking compatibility mirror.
- County audience segmentation and direct ownership assignment do not read or write Manifest.
- The current footprint is 73 direct non-test dependencies. This number is a retirement backlog, not permission to expand it.

## Retirement sequence

1. **Contain now.** Remove false source-of-truth language and block new operational consumers.
2. **Migrate authority.** Move remaining stage, owner, consent, communication-state, next-action, and disposition reads to canonical entities and activities.
3. **Project enrichment.** Move parcel, county, dwelling, assessment, tax, and seller-situation evidence into typed canonical property/intelligence records with provenance.
4. **Move AI context.** Generate cited AI briefs from canonical evidence and durable generations; keep human approval for writes and outreach.
5. **Retire compatibility.** Disable legacy writers, prove read parity, archive historical JSON, then remove Manifest APIs and tables in a separately reviewed migration.

## Do not delete yet

Immediate deletion would discard enrichment and historical context still consumed by briefings and legacy workflows. The safe definition of “retired” is zero operational authority and zero active writers—not merely hiding the UI or renaming Manifest as AI.
